# Product Rules

1. Socratic tutoring before direct answers.
2. A student mistake is a learning signal.
3. Do not trust model-generated math without verification where deterministic verification is feasible.
4. Do not update mastery from a single low-confidence model judgment.
5. Tutor progression should be explicit and testable.
6. Avoid collecting unnecessary student data.
7. Optimize MVP for measurable learning behavior, not feature count.
8. Do not add voice, mobile, whiteboard or broad subject coverage until the core tutor loop passes evals.
