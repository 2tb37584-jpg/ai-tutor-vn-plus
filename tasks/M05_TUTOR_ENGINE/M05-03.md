# M05-03 — Hint Escalation Policy

Status: BACKLOG
Codex: YES

## Goal
Define when the tutor moves from ASK_ATTEMPT → HINT_1 → HINT_2 → EXPLAIN_STEP.

## Product rule
A wrong attempt is evidence, not a trigger to immediately reveal the solution.

## Acceptance direction
Escalation is deterministic, bounded, testable and does not expose the expected final answer early.
