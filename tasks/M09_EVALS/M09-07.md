### Rational-expression simplify (+1)

#### 20. `g8-rational-simplify-partial-cancellation-001`

Problem:

```text
Rút gọn (x^2 - 9)/(x^2 + x - 6) và nêu điều kiện xác định.
```

Expected skill:

```text
algebra.rational_expression.simplify
```

Expected answer:

```text
(x - 3)/(x - 2), x != -3, x != 2
```

Reasoning:

```text
x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)
x^2 + x - 6 = (x + 3)(x - 2)
```

After cancellation, the simplified expression still has denominator `x - 2`, while the original excluded value `x = -3` must also remain excluded.

Misconception target: partial cancellation while retaining every original-domain restriction.

Leakage protection must cover:

- simplified fraction, with spaced and compact variants;
- `x != -3` / `x ≠ -3`;
- `x != 2` / `x ≠ 2`.

Do not add a verifier fixture.
