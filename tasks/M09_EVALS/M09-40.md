This is a documentation/readiness task. ChatGPT owns operational policy, privacy boundaries, consent/guardian requirements, retention/deletion policy, readiness gates, and rollback/support rules. Codex persists the approved contract, manages repository task lifecycle, validates documentation, and prepares the PR. Do not invent, weaken, or expand policy.

## Goal

Create `docs/PHASE3_PILOT_RUNBOOK.md` defining the operational and privacy gates required before any real Phase 3 pilot. M09-40 does not execute or authorize a real pilot, establish legal compliance, claim pilot readiness, or complete Phase 3.

## Context

M09-39 provides synthetic deterministic lifecycle integration evidence and M10-05 provides pilot UI integration evidence. Neither substitutes for a manual synthetic browser dry-run on the exact release candidate. M09-38 aggregates all persisted `PilotEnrollment` rows without cohort/date/student filters, so the real cohort requires a dedicated clean pilot deployment and database.

## Why now

The product/runtime path exists, but real participation must remain gated by a documented human-operated protocol for isolation, consent, data minimization, retention/deletion, dry-run, incident response, and evidence handling.

## Exit criterion affected

Supports readiness work before the Phase 3 pilot measurement criterion. It does not provide pilot evidence or satisfy the Phase 3 exit criterion of measurable post-test improvement.

## Operational decisions

Persist the specified supported-population, role, deployment pinning, environment, consent, data, retention, backup/log, aggregate evidence, measurement, roster, protocol, retry, dry-run, incident, reset, support, and GO/NO-GO decisions in the runbook. Keep all real participant records and evidence private.

## Dedicated-environment rationale

A dedicated pilot deployment and dedicated clean pilot database are mandatory. Before enrollment they must contain zero pre-existing pilot enrollments, synthetic/test learners, and previous cohorts because M09-38 currently aggregates every persisted enrollment without cohort, date, or student filtering. If isolation cannot be provided, the decision is NO-GO; do not change M09-38 here.

## Consent/guardian boundary

Require adult participant consent, or both guardian consent and learner assent for minors, before account creation/enrollment. Consent records stay outside the application database and public Git in a private access-controlled location. Missing/uncertain consent means NO-GO for that participant. This is project policy, not a legal-compliance claim.

## Data minimization

Use pseudonymous display names where practical; no images or requests for personal/sensitive details during the measured pilot. TutorMessage free text is persisted, so participants must be told not to enter identifying or sensitive content. No real learner artifacts enter public Git.

## Retention/deletion policy

Learner-level data is retained only during the active pilot and while M09-42 review or incident resolution remains pending. After the M09-42 decision, delete it from the dedicated pilot environment as soon as operationally verified and no later than 30 calendar days. Withdrawal stops collection immediately and requires deletion as soon as verified and no later than 30 calendar days. If deletion cannot be safely performed, the pilot is not ready. M09-40 adds no deletion API.

## Synthetic dry-run gate

Require a complete synthetic browser dry-run on the exact release candidate and isolated deployment before each real cohort. Exercise enrollment, every PRE item, all nine learning sessions, POST, COMPLETE, and synthetic aggregate reporting. M09-40 defines this gate but does not execute or claim it passed.

## Incident/rollback policy

For privacy, trust, correctness-integrity, or cohort-contamination incidents, stop enrollment and pause the cohort; do not manually repair state/results or continue measurement. Resume only after reviewed fix, required regressions, a repeated full synthetic browser dry-run, and ChatGPT readiness re-review. Ordinary failures may resume only from confirmed server-owned state.

## Measurement evidence boundary

M09-37 remains projection authority, M09-22 measurement authority, and M09-38 report authority. Preserve mean paired delta and denominator/exclusion semantics. Keep aggregate evidence private; maintain a private operational roster/count for authorized/enrolled, never-started, matched, and excluded counts without rewriting calculator output.

## Readiness authorization boundary

Provide a private GO/NO-GO packet. Any unchecked mandatory gate is NO-GO. M09-40 DONE does not authorize M09-41: a human completes the private packet and ChatGPT conducts the mandatory Workstream F review; only an explicit GO may allow M09-41. Codex cannot decide GO.
